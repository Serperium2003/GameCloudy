* Name: * Cloudy
* Concept:* A 3D platformer where you control a dirt golem sent to buy seeds for its creator in a nearby town, only to be detained by the king and his guards for unknown reasons. However, while you are imprisoned, the entire castle, town, and surroundings start floating and being destroyed. Freed from jail, your mission is to reach the castle to discover what happened and stop the destruction so you can finally buy the seeds.
* Target Platform: * PC (Desktop)
* Mechanics:* 
  * Jump between floating islands using magical equipment.
  * Magic Hat: * Bounce on it to perform higher jumps.
  * Magic Cape: * Transforms into a solid floating platform.
* Game Engine: * Unity (C#)
